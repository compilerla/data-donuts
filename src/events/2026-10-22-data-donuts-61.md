## Open Loop Payments Pilot

This month, Gabriela Saravia will join us to discuss results from Metrolink's Open Loop Payments Pilot.

### About Gabriela Saravia

Gabriela Saravia is a technology and fare collection leader with extensive experience managing transformative transportation initiatives and customer-facing technology solutions. As a product and program management professional, she specializes in leading cross-functional teams, driving strategic technology deployments, and delivering measurable improvements in customer experience and operational performance. Gabriela has successfully managed complex initiatives involving contactless fare payment systems, vendor partnerships, stakeholder engagement, and business process optimization. Her expertise spans product strategy, procurement, Agile delivery, performance measurement, and organizational change management, with a strong focus on turning innovative ideas into practical, scalable solutions.
